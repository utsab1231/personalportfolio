# Design System & Anti-Slop Guidelines

## Anti-AI-Slop Directives (STRICT)
- NO purple/indigo/violet gradients, dark-mode neon glows, or floating 3D/glassmorphism shapes.
- NO default Inter font, uniform rounded border cards, or heavy background drop-shadows.
- NO generic hero subtext like "Building digital experiences for tomorrow".
- NO repetitive 3-column feature cards with colorful icon badges.
- Content, hierarchy, and whitespace MUST dictate the layout—not decorative wrapping boxes.

## Visual Identity & Typography
- Theme: Clean, technical, minimal, and high-contrast (developer/engineer aesthetic).
- Palette: Off-white/light gray (`#FBFBFB` / `#F4F4F5`), high-contrast dark text (`#09090B`), subtle borders (`#E4E4E7`), and a single restrained accent color (e.g., `#0F172A` or warm neutral tones).
- Display/Headings Font: `Newsreader` (Serif) or `Space Grotesk` (Technical Sans).
- Body/Code Font: `Geist` or `JetBrains Mono`.

## Layout & Micro-interactions
- Grid & Spacing: Tight alignments, clean horizontal borders (`border-b border-zinc-200`), asymmetric structures, and clear typographic scale.
- Interactions: Fast, subtle transitions (opacity/border-color shift on hover, 100-150ms). No dramatic hover scaling or floating motion.
